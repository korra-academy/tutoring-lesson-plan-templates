# Tutoring Lesson Plan Templates (free, Markdown)

Free **lesson plan, first-session assessment, parent summary and progress report templates for tutors** — private tutors, online tutors and tutoring companies. Plain Markdown: copy, fill in, or convert to Google Docs/Word in seconds.

Maintained by [Korra Academy](https://korra.academy/) — the team behind **[Korra.Tutor](https://korra.one/)**, live 1-to-1 online tutoring and upskilling.

## How to use them

1. Copy the raw Markdown into your notes app, or clone the repo and duplicate per student.
2. Keep one folder per student: `assessment.md` once, `lesson-YYYY-MM-DD.md` per session, `report-YYYY-MM.md` per month.
3. Fill the plan in **before** the session (5 minutes), the "what happened" line **after** it (1 minute). That 6-minute habit is the whole system.

## Why plan at all? (the 5-minute argument)

A tutoring hour is expensive attention. A written objective ("by the end, the student can factorise quadratics with a coefficient of 1 unaided") turns a nice chat into measurable progress, gives parents evidence when they ask "is this working?", and gives *you* continuity between sessions — especially past 10+ students, when memory stops scaling.

## FAQ

**What should a tutoring lesson plan include?**
One objective in "student can…" form, a 5-minute warm-up retrieving last session, new material, majority-of-the-time guided practice, a 5-minute review against the objective, and optional homework. That structure is exactly [`lesson-plan.md`](templates/lesson-plan.md).

**How do I structure a first tutoring session?**
Diagnose, don't teach: goals (student's AND parent's), a low-pressure baseline task, how they've been taught before, then agree the plan and logistics. Template: [`first-session-assessment.md`](templates/first-session-assessment.md).

**What should I tell parents after each tutoring session?**
Five lines: the topic in plain words, one specific win with evidence, one "still tricky" framed as next week's focus, the homework with a due date, and the next session time. Send it within the hour. Template: [`parent-session-summary.md`](templates/parent-session-summary.md).

**How do I track a tutoring student's progress across a whole course?**
Keep one topic tracker per student per subject: every topic on the exam specification in a grid, rated red, amber or green on evidence of what the student actually did (score, unaided or not, date), with green requiring two correct unaided attempts on different days. Update it in the last minute of each session and read it in the first minute of the next; the evidence column becomes your progress report. Template: [`topic-tracker.md`](templates/topic-tracker.md).

**How often should tutors send progress reports?**
Monthly for weekly students, or per school term. Short and evidenced beats long and vague — three wins, one focus area, one ask of the parents.
