# Topic Tracker — [Student], [Subject]

One sheet per student per subject. Every topic on the course, how confident the student is right now, and the evidence behind that rating. Update it in the last minute of each session; read it in the first minute of the next one.

**Student:** ______  **Subject / level:** ______  **Exam board & date:** ______  **Started tracking:** ______

## The grid

Rate each topic with a traffic light, **based on something the student did**, not on how the session felt:

- 🔴 **Red** — can't start the question, or gets it wrong even with hints
- 🟠 **Amber** — gets there with prompting, or gets it right slowly and unreliably
- 🟢 **Green** — correct, unaided, under exam conditions, twice on different days

Add rows until every topic on the specification is listed — including the ones you haven't taught yet, marked 🔴. The empty rows are the plan.

## How to fill it in

1. **Session 1:** list every topic from the exam specification (the board publishes it free). Rate the ones the baseline task touched in [`first-session-assessment.md`](first-session-assessment.md); leave the rest 🔴.
2. **End of every session (1 minute):** change the rating for the topic you worked on *only if you have evidence*. Write the evidence in words a parent could read: "6/8 on mixed past-paper questions, unaided, 14 Oct".
3. **Start of every session:** scan the column. Reds nearest the exam get taught; ambers get retrieval practice in the warm-up; greens get one question a month so they stay green.
4. **Before writing a report:** the Evidence column *is* the report. Copy three greens and one amber straight into [`progress-report.md`](progress-report.md).

## Rules that keep the tracker honest

- **Green needs two dates.** One good session is a mood, not mastery. If it was right once, it's amber with a note to re-test next week.
- **Ratings go down as well as up.** A green that fails a cold retrieval question becomes amber again. Nobody is offended by a tracker; they are offended by an exam result nobody predicted.
- **Evidence beats adjectives.** "Confident with this now" is not evidence. "Did Q4 and Q7 from the June paper, full marks, no hints" is.
- **Student sees it.** Show the grid at the end of the session and ask them where they'd put themselves. Where you disagree is the most useful conversation you'll have that week.
- **Red is not a judgement.** Early in the course most of the sheet should be red — that's the course, not the student. The sheet exists so you can watch it change colour.

## Reading the sheet before an exam

Six weeks out, count the colours. If more than a third of the sheet is still red, the session plan is too slow: switch to [`exam-countdown-planner.md`](exam-countdown-planner.md) and triage by marks available. If it's mostly amber, the job is past papers, not teaching. If it's mostly green, the job is timed practice and keeping it green.
